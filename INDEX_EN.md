# Shukaku Hub

## An app to help with direct-to-consumer sales

Supports order management, product management, customer management, and invoicing.

---

## About the App

Shukaku Hub is an order management app designed for sole proprietors and small-scale farmers. It lets you manage customers, products, and orders in one place, and generate invoice PDFs.

---

## Key Features

### 📝 Order Management

Register, list, and manage order details.

### 📦 Product Management

Register, edit, and manage your product catalog.

### 👤 Customer Management

Organize customer information for easy search and management.

### 📄 Invoicing

Generate invoice PDFs from order information.
